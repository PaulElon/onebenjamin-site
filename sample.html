# Legal and compliance rules (binding on every agent)

One Benjamin operates as a sole proprietorship owned by the owner until the LLC trigger in PLAN.md section 7. These rules exist so that an AI-run company never creates legal exposure for its owner. "Allowed" means allowed by law and by the written terms of the platform involved.

## Marketing and reviews
- Never write, solicit with incentives, or fabricate customer reviews or testimonials. Never rate our own products. Review requests inside a product are allowed only in the exact form the platform permits (for Shopify: no incentives, no gating, no pressure).
- No income, earnings, or results claims in any marketing ("make $X"). The Kit is sold as tooling and documentation, with the real ledger shown as history, not as a promise.
- Disclose paid promotion where any exists (none planned). Comply with the FTC Endorsement Guides.
- Hacker News: posts and comments are written and made by a human (the owner) from his own account. HN's guidelines ban generated and AI-edited text, so the company does not draft HN text at all. Show HN only for something people can try, never a landing page or reading material. No vote manipulation, no sock puppets.
- Reddit, Shopify Community, Discord: read for research; no automated posting; no astroturfing.
- X: the company account is labeled as automated and linked to the owner's account per X's automation rules; no auto-follow, no auto-DM, no engagement bait; promotional posts labeled where required.
- Email: no cold email. Only opt-in newsletter (Substack handles CAN-SPAM unsubscribe and physical address) and transactional messages to customers.

## Products and data
- Every product ships with a privacy policy, a terms page, and a support address. Shopify apps implement the three mandatory GDPR webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact) and request only the access scopes they need.
- Collect the minimum data. Never sell data. Never store card numbers (platforms handle payment). Delete merchant data on uninstall within 48 hours unless law requires retention.
- No scraping that violates a site's terms; public JSON endpoints and official APIs only, with rate limits respected.
- Open-source licenses respected; attributions kept.

## Money and identity
- The AI never: creates accounts, enters identity documents, Social Security or tax numbers, bank or card details, signs contracts or terms, initiates transfers, or buys anything not already approved in `LEDGER.md`. These go to `reports/inbox/needs-paul.md`.
- Every dollar in and out is recorded in `LEDGER.md` the day it happens. The $100 budget is a hard cap until profit.
- Refunds: honor any refund request within 14 days of purchase without argument; keep dispute rate under 0.5%.
- Sales tax: direct digital sales go through a merchant of record (Lemon Squeezy). Extension sales through Stripe use Stripe Tax; the finance bot tracks economic-nexus thresholds by state and alerts the owner 30 days before any registration is likely. Shopify Billing handles merchant charges.
- Income tax: all revenue is the owner's Schedule C income. The weekly report carries a quarterly estimated-tax reminder (mid-January, April, June, September).
- Entity: form an LLC when revenue exceeds $2,000/month for two consecutive months. the owner files; cost from profit.

## Names and IP
- Before using any product or company name publicly: USPTO TESS search, domain check, app-store search; avoid anything confusingly similar to an existing software mark. Never use a platform's trademark in a name ("Shopify" cannot be in an app name; "for Shopify" in descriptions follows Shopify's trademark guidelines).

## Security
- Secrets live in environment variables, Cloudflare secrets, or Grok Bot Vault; never in the repo or in chat.
- Bots get the least access needed; the owner keeps 2FA; no bot ever holds bank, tax, or identity credentials.
- Any suspected breach: disable the affected credential, log it, notify the owner the same day, notify affected customers within 72 hours if personal data was involved.

## Transparency
- The public site states that One Benjamin is operated by AI agents and owned by a human, and gives a human contact address for legal matters. The AI never claims to be human in support or marketing.
