How to hide a payment method by country in Shopify

You sell in several countries but a payment method only makes sense in some of them: Cash on Delivery only where your courier collects cash, bank transfer only for domestic buyers, a method hidden where you cannot support it. Merchants ask for this often:

"How to change the payment method by country" (Shopify Community)

This guide covers what Shopify does on its own, where that stops, and how a rule at checkout fills the gap.

Short answer

Shopify lets you turn on or off local payment methods per market, if you use Shopify Payments. It has no documented per-country switch for manual methods such as Cash on Delivery or bank deposit: in Settings > Payments a manual method is on or off for the whole store. To show a manual or third-party method in some countries and hide it in others, you need a payment customization: a small rule (a Shopify Function) that runs inside checkout and removes the method when the buyer's country matches.

What Shopify does natively

  • Local payment methods, per market. If a market includes a country that is eligible for local payment methods, its market settings show a payments card where you can activate or deactivate those methods. This applies to local methods offered through Shopify Payments, not to manual methods.
  • Manual methods follow the market's currency. Manual methods display in the currency configured for each market. To accept manual payments in more than one currency, Shopify says you need Shopify Payments and its international sales tools. Shopify's manual payments page does not describe a way to limit a manual method to certain countries.
  • US stores with Shopify-managed sales tax cannot accept manual payments at all until sales tax is set to self-directed, per the same page.

Sources: Shopify Help: Markets, payments; Shopify Help: Manual payment methods. Checked 2026-11-18.

How the checkout rule works

A payment customization function receives the cart at checkout, including each delivery group's shipping address and its two-letter country code (for example US), the billing address country, and the country the store is localized for. It returns a list of operations. The ones that matter here:

  • Hide a payment method.
  • Rename it (for example "Cash on Delivery (pay the courier)").
  • Move it up or down the list.

Pick the country you check on purpose. The shipping country is where the parcel goes and where cash would be collected, so it is the usual choice for Cash on Delivery. The localized country is the storefront country the buyer is browsing in, which can differ from where they ship.

Source: shopify.dev: Payment Customization Function API (cart.deliveryGroups.deliveryAddress.countryCode, cart.billingAddress.countryCode, localization.country.isoCode; operations). Checked 2026-11-18.

Limits worth knowing

  • Plan and country restrictions on some methods. Shopify says plan and geographical restrictions apply, and operations that target a restricted payment method do not take effect. Test the exact method you want to hide on your own store before you rely on it.
  • Whole method only, unless you are on Plus. Only Plus stores can hide a method from a specific placement; on other plans the whole method is hidden.
  • Shop Pay and wallets. Functions do not apply operations to methods inside Shop Pay. Express wallets can be removed but not reordered.
  • Not in POS. Payment customization functions do not run in Shopify Point of Sale.
  • 25 per store. A store can activate at most 25 payment customization functions, across all apps.

Sources: shopify.dev: About functions in payments; Payment Customization Function API. Checked 2026-11-18.

Our app

OneBen Payment Rules hides, renames, or moves payment methods at checkout by cart subtotal, shipping country, or customer tag. For a country rule you enter two-letter codes such as GB, IE; the rule matches when the shipping address is in one of them. Until the buyer has entered a shipping address, and on orders with no shipping address, a country rule has nothing to match, so it does not fire. It does not check the billing country or the storefront country.

What Payment Rules can't hide or change

  • Draft orders completed in the admin, order edits, and POS. Shopify does not run payment customization functions there. Shopify lists draft order invoice checkouts as supported, but some merchants report payment-hiding apps not applying to invoices, and we have not tested it ourselves. Test one invoice before you rely on it.
  • Methods inside Shop Pay. Once a buyer is in Shop Pay, Shopify applies no hide, rename, or move to its payment methods.
  • Express buttons. A hide rule removes a wallet such as Apple Pay or Google Pay from every place it shows in checkout (express buttons and the payment list); the app does not hide one placement only. Wallets cannot be renamed or moved. We have not yet tested wallet rules on a real checkout.
  • Methods Shopify restricts for your plan or country. Shopify ignores rules that target a restricted method. One competitor says credit cards on non-Plus US and Canada stores are restricted; we have not verified that, so test the exact method on your store.
  • Billing country and storefront country. Country rules read the shipping address only.
