How to exclude a brand (vendor) from a Shopify discount

You want a store-wide sale, but one brand must stay at full price. Often the reason is a brand agreement, such as a minimum advertised price policy, or a supplier whose margin cannot take 20% off. Shopify stores the brand in the product's Vendor field, and that is what you can build on.

Short answer

A Shopify amount-off discount has no "exclude" field. Under Applies to you can pick Specific products or Specific collections. So you build an automated collection of every product except that vendor, and point the discount at the collection. Vendor is one of the four condition types Shopify allows in a collection's Exclude section.

The native workaround, step by step

  1. Check the exact vendor name. Open one of the brand's products and copy the Vendor value. The is equal to condition must exactly match the whole value, so "Acme" and "Acme Co." are different vendors.
  2. Create a collection called "Discount eligible". In Products > Collections, add a Products source that includes your products by condition (for example, product status is equal to Active).
  3. Exclude the brand. In that source, open Exclude and add Vendor is equal to the name you copied. To protect several brands, add one vendor condition per brand. Shopify says exclusions always override inclusions.
  4. Point the discount at it. Create or edit the discount, set Applies to to Specific collections, and choose "Discount eligible". A code you already shared keeps working after you edit it.

Because the collection is automated, new products from other vendors join it on their own, and new products from the protected brand stay out, as long as their Vendor field is spelled the same way.

Limits of the workaround

LimitWhat it means
Exact spellingIs equal to needs the whole value. If imports have left the brand under two spellings, either add both, or use contains (at least 3 characters, no leading or trailing spaces), which can also catch other vendors whose names share those letters.
"Match any" vs "Match all"Shopify's own troubleshooting: with all conditions, a product must match none of the exclusion criteria; with any condition, a product is included if it fails at least one exclusion criterion. With two or more brand exclusions, "any" can let a protected brand back in. Use "Match all".
60 condition values per sourcePlenty for a few brands, tight if you protect a long list of vendors.
One collection per exclusion setA sale that excludes brand A and a code that excludes brand B need two "eligible" collections.
Codes set to all productsThe exclusion does nothing until each existing discount is switched to Specific collections.
StackingWhether the discount combines with other discounts is a separate setting, in its Combinations section.

If you also need to keep items already on sale out of the discount, see How to exclude products from a Shopify discount, which covers the compare-at price catches.
