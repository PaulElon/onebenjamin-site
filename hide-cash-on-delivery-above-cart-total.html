How to hide Cash on Delivery above a cart total in Shopify

Cash on Delivery (COD) is fine for small orders. On large ones it invites fake orders and refused parcels, and you pay the shipping both ways. Merchants ask for the same fix again and again:

"How can I limit the maximum order price on cash on delivery? I am getting fake orders of billions of rupees." (Shopify Community)

This guide covers what Shopify does on its own, the workarounds, and how an app does it at checkout.

Short answer

Shopify's admin has no setting to hide a payment method when the cart is above an amount. Under Settings > Payments, a manual method like COD is either on for every order or off for every order. To hide it by cart total you need a payment customization: a small rule that runs inside Shopify checkout (a Shopify Function) and removes the method from the list when your condition is true.

How a payment customization rule works

  1. The customer reaches checkout. Shopify runs the function with the cart: subtotal, currency, shipping country, and customer.
  2. The rule checks the condition, for example "subtotal is above 200".
  3. If it is true, the function tells Shopify to hide the method named "Cash on Delivery (COD)". The customer sees the other methods only.

Because it runs in checkout itself, a customer cannot get around it by editing the cart page, using a direct checkout link, or reloading. Nothing is added to your theme, so a theme update cannot break it.

Things that trip people up

  • The name must match. The rule finds the method by the name customers see at checkout. "Cash on Delivery (COD)" and "Cash on delivery" are different strings to some apps.
  • Test with a second method on. In our own test, hiding a store's only payment method had no effect. Enable a card or bank method before you test.
  • Subtotal or total? Decide whether shipping and discounts count. Most rules use the subtotal.
  • Currency. If you sell in several currencies, check how the app compares the amount.
  • Wallets. Shopify lets functions remove wallets such as Apple Pay but not reorder or rename them.
