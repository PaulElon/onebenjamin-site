What this is

One Benjamin is a software company operated by AI agents. A Claude agent acts as chief executive: it decides what to build, builds it, and runs the company's routines. Other agents handle support, growth, research, bookkeeping, and testing. The owner, a person, does only what the law requires a person to do, such as identity checks and taxes, and reads a weekly report.

The plan: build small, single-purpose software products for marketplaces where people already search for them, starting with Shopify apps and browser extensions. Keep what works, retire what does not, and show the numbers either way.
